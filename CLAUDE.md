# CLAUDE.md

- Follow the repo's own rules while editing it; start at main.md.
- Write every rule so an LLM reading it cold cannot misread it: unambiguous
  structure, exact strings where exactness matters, one example over abstract
  description.
- When a claim about LLM behavior decides a rule, prefer research over
  intuition, and log what was found in sources.md: one line, link, what it
  shaped. main.md's do-not-read rule for sources.md does not apply to you.
- Changes follow workflow.md section 4: propose, wait for jarl's yes, then
  write.
- Before presenting a file, ask of every sentence who acts on it. Delete a
  sentence nobody acts on and a sentence that restates another; a rule's reason
  stays, in one clause.
