# Sources

Where the rules came from. Bookkeeping for jarl; not for models to read.

- "I make Claude Code keep a MISTAKES.md file" (thabxi, r/ClaudeCode, 2026-08):
  shaped the mistakes-log convention in workflow.md.
- "Test behavior, not implementation" (TestDriven.io): backs the testing section
  in code-style.md.
- "An Empirical Evaluation of Property-Based Testing in Python"
  (https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~mcoblenz/assets/pdf/OOPSLA_2025_PBT.pdf): the
  property-based-testing candidate, not yet adopted.
- Coverage vs mutation vs property-based overview
  (https://www.aravindhu.com/software%20development/2020/12/06/coverage-vs-mutation-vs-property-base-testing.html):
  the planted-bug test-quality measure, not yet adopted.
- ponytail (https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail): distilled into the
  check-in-order bullet in code-style.md.
- GitClear 211M-line analysis (via
  https://arter.dev/blog/the-antidote-to-code-slop/): motivated the anti-slop
  rule families in ruff.toml.
- antislop (https://github.com/skew202/antislop): its placeholder category
  became FIX in ruff.toml.
- scicode-lint (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17893): watched, not adopted.
- "Brevity is the soul of sustainability" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.08686):
  basis for the minimal-sufficient-answer rule in main.md.
- YapBench (https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00624): the "minimal sufficient"
  phrasing in main.md.
- Concise-thought accuracy cost (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.05618): why the
  answer rule keeps the explanation escape hatch.
- Contrastive Chain-of-Thought (https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09277): why
  language.md teaches with don't/do pairs, kept few.
